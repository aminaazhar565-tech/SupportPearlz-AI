# Shipping Policy
## Delivery timelines
Metro locations normally receive standard delivery in 2-3 working days. Secondary cities normally receive delivery in 3-5 working days. Remote areas normally receive delivery in 5-7 working days.

## Same-day dispatch
Orders placed before 2:00 PM on working days normally enter same-day dispatch. Orders after the cut-off move to the next working day.

## Next-day delivery
Next-day delivery is offered only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when the option is available at checkout. It is not offered as a standard service to remote areas.

## Failed delivery
After a failed delivery attempt, the courier may retry. Customers should use the tracking information to follow the shipment status.

## International shipping
International shipping is not currently documented as an available standard service in this policy.
