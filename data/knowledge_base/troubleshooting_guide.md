# Troubleshooting Guide
## Three beeps and red light
Three short beeps with a blinking red light indicate E-03: sediment pre-filter saturation. Replace the sediment pre-filter and hold RESET for five seconds.

## No power
Check the supplied adapter and socket. If the cable is damaged or there is sparking, stop and contact support. Do not open the electrical housing.

## Low flow
Check whether the filter is due for replacement and verify that the inlet hose is not kinked. If low flow remains after the basic checks, escalate to support.

## Leaking base
Turn off the unit, disconnect power, and check external hose connections. Do not open the internal housing.

## Bad taste after filter change
Complete the first-run flushing procedure in the installation guide. If the taste remains unusual after the documented flush, contact support.

## App will not pair
Confirm Bluetooth is enabled and the purifier is powered. If pairing still fails, restart the app and follow the pairing steps. Escalate if the issue persists.

## High TDS reading
Check the display again after the unit has completed its normal startup process. If the reading remains outside the product documentation's stated range, contact support.

## Noisy pump
A temporary change in pump sound can occur during operation. Persistent unusual noise should be escalated rather than repaired by the customer.

## Safety escalation
Customers must not open the electrical housing, bypass safety devices, repair the pump, or modify wiring themselves.
