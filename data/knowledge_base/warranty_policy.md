# Pearlz Warranty Policy
## 1. Warranty period
AquaPearl 500 Pro is covered for 24 months from the invoice date. The delivery date is not used to calculate warranty duration.

## 2. Covered components
The standard warranty covers the purifier body, pump and electronics when the failure results from normal household use.

## 3. Exclusions
Filter cartridges are consumables and are not covered. Physical damage, unauthorised repair, commercial use and damage caused by unsuitable water quality are excluded.

## 4. Resale
Warranty transferability is not guaranteed on resale unless the original purchase record and transfer conditions in the service system are satisfied.

## 5. Claim documents
A customer requesting warranty service should provide the purchase invoice and product serial number. The service team may request photographs when useful for diagnosis.
