# AquaPearl 500 Pro Product Manual
## Product overview
The AquaPearl 500 Pro is a countertop smart water purifier. The box contains the purifier, power adapter, inlet hose, drain hose, quick-start card and filter set.

## Technical specifications
Flow rate is up to 2.5 litres per minute. Internal tank capacity is 4 litres. Rated power is 65 watts. The unit uses sediment, carbon, membrane and polishing filtration stages.

## Control panel
A solid green light means normal operation. A blinking red light indicates an alert. Three short beeps indicate E-03. E-03 means the sediment pre-filter has reached saturation.

## Filter maintenance
Replace the sediment pre-filter every 6 months under normal household use. After replacement, hold RESET for five seconds to clear the filter alert.

## Cleaning
Wipe the exterior with a soft damp cloth. Do not immerse the electrical body. Run the descaling procedure every 3 months in normal use, following the descaling instructions supplied with the unit.

## Safety
Use the supplied power adapter. Do not open the electrical housing. Stop operation and contact support if the unit has a damaged power cable, burning smell or electrical sparking.
