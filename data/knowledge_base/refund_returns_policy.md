# Refund & Returns Policy
## 1. Return window
Unopened products may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery.

## 2. Opened products
Opened products are eligible only when the product is defective on arrival and the defect is reported within 7 calendar days. Normal change-of-mind returns after opening are not accepted.

## 3. Shipping
For an eligible unopened return, Pearlz provides the return shipping label. For non-defective change-of-mind returns, the customer pays return shipping.

## 4. Refund processing
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Card refunds may take additional bank processing time.

## 5. Bundles
Promotional bundles must be returned as a complete bundle unless support authorises a partial return.
