# Service & Maintenance Agreement
## Annual plans
The Standard Care plan includes one scheduled maintenance visit per year. The Plus Care plan includes two scheduled visits per year and priority scheduling.

## Response times
Standard Care targets a response within 3 working days. Plus Care targets a response within 1 working day.

## Parts
Routine filter consumables are billed separately unless a promotion explicitly states otherwise. Covered warranty repairs follow the warranty policy.

## Warranty claim procedure
For a warranty claim, the customer first submits the invoice and serial number. The service team reviews eligibility and may request photos or diagnostic information. If approved, the service team schedules the next step.

## Cancellation
Annual maintenance plans may be cancelled according to the plan terms. Any pro-rata refund is calculated by the service team; this document does not specify a universal refund formula.
