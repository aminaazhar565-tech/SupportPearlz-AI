# Privacy Policy
## Purchase data
Pearlz collects information needed to process an order, such as name, delivery details, contact details and payment transaction references.

## Mobile app data
The mobile app may collect purifier status and water-usage telemetry to provide device features and diagnostics.

## Retention
Purchase records are retained for the period required for service, accounting and legal obligations. The exact retention duration for each record class is not specified in this summary.

## Sharing
Relevant information may be shared with couriers and payment processors to complete requested services.

## User rights
Customers may request access, deletion or export of eligible personal data through the documented privacy request process.

## Cookies
The online store uses cookies for essential functionality and session preferences.
