# Installation Guide
## Before installation
Water pressure should be between 1.5 and 4 bar. Use a grounded electrical socket and leave clearance around the unit for ventilation and filter access.

## First-run flushing
After installing a new filter, run the unit through the first-run flush cycle described in the quick-start procedure. The flush removes manufacturing dust and residual carbon fines. Water may taste unusual until the flush cycle is completed.

## Wall-mount installation
Mark the mounting points, secure the bracket with suitable anchors, connect the inlet and drain lines, and check for leaks before operation.

## Under-sink installation
Confirm cabinet clearance, route the inlet and drain hoses without sharp bends, connect the power adapter, then perform the first-run flush.

## Professional installation
Professional installation is available as a paid service. The exact booking price is not specified in this guide.

## Warranty caution
Do not modify electrical components or bypass safety devices during self-installation.
