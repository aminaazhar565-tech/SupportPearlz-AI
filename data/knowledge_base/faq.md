# Frequently Asked Questions
## How long is the warranty?
The AquaPearl 500 Pro standard warranty is 24 months from the invoice date.

## What is in the box?
The box contains the purifier, power adapter, inlet hose, drain hose, quick-start card and filter set.

## How often should I replace the sediment filter?
The normal replacement interval is 6 months.

## Can I return an unopened product?
Yes, an unopened product may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery.

## Can I return a product after opening it?
Only a defective-on-arrival opened product reported within 7 calendar days qualifies under the documented opened-product rule.

## How long does standard delivery take?
Metro: 2-3 working days; secondary cities: 3-5; remote areas: 5-7.

## Is next-day delivery available everywhere?
No. The shipping policy lists next-day delivery for Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when offered at checkout.

## How do I reset E-03?
Replace the sediment pre-filter and hold RESET for five seconds.

## Why does water taste unusual after a filter change?
Complete the first-run flushing procedure.

## Do you offer student discounts?
This knowledge base does not document a student discount.

## Can I pay in instalments?
This knowledge base does not document an instalment payment program.
