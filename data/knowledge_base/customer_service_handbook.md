# Customer Service Handbook
## Support hours
Support is available Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.

## Channels
Customers may use the web contact form, email support channel, or WhatsApp support channel configured by Pearlz.

## Escalation
Electrical safety issues, suspected internal leaks, damaged power cables and repeated unresolved failures should be escalated to a senior support agent.

## Tone
Support responses should be concise, factual and respectful. Agents must not promise compensation, delivery exceptions or warranty coverage that the documentation does not support.

## Complaints
Record the customer's issue, relevant order or serial information when available, and the policy source used for the response.
